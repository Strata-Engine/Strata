# SDD ledger — plan: docs/2026-11-11-animation-plan.md
Spec: docs/2026-11-11-animation-design.md. Branch claude/strata-animation-support-310347.
Task 1: Ruling: a clip's turn key is the part's whole local rotation (it replaces the rest rotation), and a move key is the whole offset from the pivot; a part with no keys in any active layer holds its rest rotation and no movement — the test's "0 to 90 degrees about z is a 45-degree turn at 0.5" reads that way on a rig at rest — a clip made against one rest pose does not carry over to a rig with a different rest pose.
Task 1: Ruling: a track belongs to a part, not to a channel: a part with turn keys but no move keys takes movement 0 from that clip (and one with move keys only takes its rest rotation), weighted like the rest of the layer — a layer that leaves a part's movement to the layers below it needs no move keys, but it cannot keep the movement while it turns — a turn-only clip over a bobbing layer stops the bob in proportion to its weight.
Task 1: Ruling: a layer holds up to 3 sources (a clip, or a blend of up to 4 clips): the newest ramps 0 to 1 over the fade while each older one ramps from its share when the newest began down to 0, so the shares always sum to 1; a fourth play during a fade pushes out the faintest source and the rest are renormalised — the outgoing clips keep running and firing marks — three plays inside one fade pop by the faintest source's share (it was 4 sources; cut to 3 for memory, see the fix below).
Task 1: Ruling: mask and mode (override or additive) belong to the source, not the layer — a crossfade between clips of different masks or modes stays continuous — none.
Task 1: Ruling: a layer moves a part by its weight times the share of its sources that have keys for that part; sources that have none leave it to the layers below — so a crossfade into a clip that does not touch a part fades that part back to what is below, and a blend whose neighbours move different parts moves each by its own share — none.
Task 1: Ruling: an additive source's delta is sample(t) times the inverse of the track's first key (turn), and the move less the first key's move; it is scaled from identity by the layer weight and multiplied on the left of what is below (in the parent's frame) — the controller's resolution of the brief — none.
Task 1: Ruling: an_play on an empty layer starts its clip at full share and ramps the layer weight from 0 over the fade; on a playing layer it crossfades the sources and ramps the layer weight to the new weight over the same fade — a single ramp rule — the layer weight and the crossfade share one fade time.
Task 1: Ruling: a mask id below 0 (pass -1) means every part; mask ids from an_mask start at 0 — 0 is a valid id — the March binding must map "all parts" to -1.
Task 1: Ruling: a blend keeps one phase 0..1 advanced at rate / (the neighbours' durations lerped by the value); each clip is sampled at phase times its own duration; it loops only if every clip in it loops; an_set time on a blend sets the phase (0..1), on a clip the time in seconds; marks come from the two neighbours only, weighted by layer weight, source share and neighbour share — the brief's "keep their phase equal" — a blend of walk and run whose tags mean the same footstep fires both, the fainter at lower weight.
Task 1: Ruling: marks cross on (a, b] going forward and [b, a) going back, and a step that starts a clip (play, set time) or starts after a wrap includes its start; on a loop a mark at the duration is the mark at 0; an update ending exactly on the wrap leaves the mark at 0 to the next update — each crossing fires once, a mark at 0 fires at the start of play — none.
Task 1: Ruling: one update walks at most 64 loops of a clip for marks (whole loops past that are skipped) and reports at most 8 marks per figure (the buffer is capacity x 8); marks past either cap are counted in stat 6 (marks dropped); a duration-0 clip never advances and fires no marks; a key outside 0..duration is clamped into it; two keys at one time are a jump (the later one holds from that time) — a dt of thousands of loops would otherwise walk every loop, and a per-figure share keeps the loss from falling on the figures in the last slots (review finding) — within one figure the marks kept are those found first (lower layers, older sources), not the earliest in time.
Task 1: Ruling: a negative or non-finite dt changes nothing (marks of the last update stay) and is counted in stat 4 — the brief counts negative dt only — a NaN dt shows as a negative one.
Task 1: Ruling: figure ids are a counter mod 2^20 (skipping ids still live), looked up through an open-addressing table of 2 x capacity entries (rounded up to a power of two; linear probing, backward-shift deletion); slots are reused lowest-freed-last (a stack) — the brief's "reused only after 2^20 spawns" without a 4 MB table (review finding) — a lookup probes instead of indexing; checked by a scratch run of 1.5 million random spawns and removes.
Task 1: Ruling: an_spawn poses the rest pose at once, so a new figure's matrices are never garbage; a figure placed or played since its last pose is stale (stat 2) until the next update — none.
Task 1: Ruling: an_hash runs over each live figure's id and its parts' matrices in slot order; springs join it in Task 3 — none.
Task 1: Ruling: an_rig requires exactly one root, at index 0 (parent -1), and every other parent before its child — the brief's parent-first order; one forward pass poses the hierarchy — a rig of two separate trees needs a pure-joint root.
Task 1: Ruling: fixed tables per world: 256 rigs, 4 096 masks, 4 096 clips, 262 144 keys (at most 65 535 in one clip), 32 768 marks; a full table refuses with reason 8 — every array is allocated in an_open, and the design's 48 MB budget must hold two poses and Tasks 3-4's state — a game with more than 256 rigs or 262 144 keys needs the constants raised.
Task 1: Ruling: the crossfade test's target clip turns the arm to 30 degrees (the hand to 30), not further — with a linear 0.2 s fade at 60 updates a second, a pose 90 degrees away moves the hand 0.13 m in one update at its 1 m lever, so the 0.1 bound is a bound on the test's poses as much as on the fade — fades between very different poses still move more than 0.1 an update.
Task 1 fix: Ruling: marks past the 64-loop skip and past a figure's share are counted in an_stat(6) — the design says every crossed mark is recorded and nothing is dropped silently (review finding 1) — none.

Task 2: Ruling (Step 1 probe): `forge test` in `animcheck/` does link the app's `[ffi]` sources — a probe test calling `Strata.Ffi.Anim.open` passed (it needed `needs IO.Console` beside IO.Clock and IO.Process) — C-backed checks still run as animcheck scenes through `scripts/anim_check.sh`, as the brief says, and `test/*_test.march` test only pure modules; the probe test was removed — none.
Task 2: Ruling: a part's frame has its origin at its anchor, a point of its own grid in its own voxels (default (0, 0, 0), the grid's low corner; `Strata.Rig.anchor` sets it); voxel (x, y, z) spans [x, x+1] less the anchor; the pivot is the anchor's place in the parent's grid, in the parent's voxels, so the kernel's pivot is (pivot - parent's anchor) x voxel size and the box is (-anchor, size - anchor) x voxel size — the brief's `part` has no own-pivot argument, and an arm must turn about its shoulder, not its grid's corner — `anchor` is a call beyond the brief's list; the renderer (Task 6) must offset a part's pieces by its anchor.
Task 2: Ruling: a part may be 0 voxels on a side (a pure joint with no grid); 129 is refused — the design allows a part with no voxels — none.
Task 2: Ruling: `box` takes inclusive corners; `voxel` and `box` accept colour 0 to empty; edits are kept as a list and painted at meshing, so a voxel call never copies a grid — a grid in a record would be copied by every write — `get` paints the part's grid to read one voxel (slow, for tests and tools).
Task 2: Ruling: the part mesher draws a face only toward an empty voxel or the outside of the part (never between filled voxels of different colours), merges equal palette index and equal corner shades, leaves the flags byte 0, and omits pieces with nothing to draw; pieces come in order x, then z, then y — palette voxels are all opaque — a glass-like palette entry would need its own rule.
Task 2: Ruling: a rig's masks are numbered 0, 1, ... in the order `Strata.Rig.mask` made them; `finish_rig` uploads them all at once (an_x_masks) right after the rig so the world's ids are contiguous, and an_x_play / an_x_blend turn a rig's mask number into the world's id (-1 stays every part; a number the rig lacks is refused as AN_R_MASK) — the game should not see world-wide mask ids — none.
Task 2: Ruling: `finish_clip` finds the clip's rig among the rigs finished in this world by sameness (`Strata.Rig.same`, field by field; Rig has no Eq), newest first; a clip whose rig was never finished goes to C with rig -1 and is refused (AN_R_RIG) — the brief's `Clip.new` takes a Rig, not an id — a rig finished twice resolves to the newer id.
Task 2: Ruling: `swing` and `spin` key the rest rotation turned further about an axis in the part's rest frame (rest x turn); swing's phase is a fraction of the period; both add a key at the duration when the last step falls short of it; move keys are in metres — the kernel's turn key replaces the rest rotation (Task 1), so a helper must carry it — two helpers on one part's turn channel interleave keys rather than add.
Task 2: Ruling: `spawn` is a March keyword (GAPS S22), so a figure is made with `Strata.Anim.spawn_at`; `linear` is too, so linear interpolation is `Strata.Clip.straight()` (with `step()` and `smooth()`) — none — the design's name `spawn` cannot be used.
Task 2: Ruling: an_x_spawn spawns, places and poses at once, so a new figure's matrices are right before the first update and it is not stale — none.
Task 2: Ruling: `Strata.Anim.matrices` allocates 12 x parts floats and an_x_matrices fills them in one call, as `Strata.Ffi.Noise.field2` fills a caller-owned NativeF32Arr; marks still come back one number a call (an_x_mark_int, an_x_mark_weight) — the brief names an_x_matrices as the March path, and a call per float would be per-frame March work proportional to joints (the design's anti-goal; review finding) — a March reader still allocates one array per figure read.
Task 2: Ruling: `Strata.Anim.hash` is an_hash folded to its low 62 bits, so it is the same number as a March Int — none.
Task 2: Ruling: Strata.Rig and Strata.Clip keep rotations as four-float tuples inside lists and constructors (GAPS S23: a Quat through List.map fails to compile) — none.
Task 2: Ruling: `set_rate` and `set_time` take a fade, as the brief lists, but the kernel applies them at once — an_set has no fade for them — none.
Task 2: Ruling: `finish_rig` answers -1 when the rig is accepted but one of its masks is refused (the masks table full); the rig stays in the world, unused — a half-uploaded rig would play under the wrong masks — a full mask table wastes one rig slot per failed finish.
Task 2: Ruling: a socket's position is in its part's grid coordinates (voxels from the grid's low corner, like `voxel` and `box`), not relative to the anchor; in the part's frame it sits at (position - anchor) x voxel size — every voxel coordinate a rig takes is a grid coordinate — Task 4 must subtract the anchor when it uploads sockets.
