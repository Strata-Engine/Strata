Task 1: Ruling: a clip's turn key is the part's whole local rotation (it replaces the rest rotation), and a move key is the whole offset from the pivot; a part with no keys in any active layer holds its rest rotation and no movement — the test's "0 to 90 degrees about z is a 45-degree turn at 0.5" reads that way on a rig at rest — a clip made against one rest pose does not carry over to a rig with a different rest pose.
Task 1: Ruling: a track belongs to a part, not to a channel: a part with turn keys but no move keys takes movement 0 from that clip (and one with move keys only takes its rest rotation), weighted like the rest of the layer — a layer that leaves a part's movement to the layers below it needs no move keys, but it cannot keep the movement while it turns — a turn-only clip over a bobbing layer stops the bob in proportion to its weight.
Task 1: Ruling: a layer holds up to 3 sources (a clip, or a blend of up to 4 clips): the newest ramps 0 to 1 over the fade while each older one ramps from its share when the newest began down to 0, so the shares always sum to 1; a fourth play during a fade pushes out the faintest source and the rest are renormalised — the outgoing clips keep running and firing marks — three plays inside one fade pop by the faintest source's share (it was 4 sources; cut to 3 for memory, see the fix below).
Task 1: Ruling: mask and mode (override or additive) belong to the source, not the layer — a crossfade between clips of different masks or modes stays continuous — none.
Task 1: Ruling: a layer moves a part by its weight times the share of its sources that have keys for that part; sources that have none leave it to the layers below — so a crossfade into a clip that does not touch a part fades that part back to what is below, and a blend whose neighbours move different parts moves each by its own share — none.
Task 1: Ruling: an additive source's delta is sample(t) times the inverse of the track's first key (turn), and the move less the first key's move; it is scaled from identity by the layer weight and multiplied on the left of what is below (in the parent's frame) — the controller's resolution of the brief — none.
