# Rulings made while building strata_kit
- Task 1: Ruling: a replaced grid is a second kind of edit, `RgBase(grid)`, at the start of the part's edit history (the rest of the rig's code is unchanged); painting copies it. Cost if wrong: none outside rig.march.
- Task 2: Ruling: replacing keeps the rig's id, so figures need no re-rig; their per-part animation state (layers, procedural records) is cleared and they are posed at rest, and figures hanging from them are unhung where they stand (their socket may be gone). The design's "re-rig a figure without losing its animation state" is met for placement, drop and attachment as a child; layer state cannot survive renumbered parts. Cost if wrong: a game that wanted clips to keep playing across a swap must restart them.
- Task 2: Ruling: freeing compacts the grid and brick pools in place (they are laid down in the order made, so one forward pass moves every kept grid down). Masks and clips of a freed or replaced rig are not reclaimed (strata_kit uses neither). Cost: a game that replaces rigs with masks thousands of times exhausts the 4096 masks.
- Task 2: 1000 replacements of 25-part rigs (4 x 4 x 4 voxels a part) in 68 ms; bricks steady at 300. Stats 12 (live rigs), 13 (grids), 14 (bricks) added.
- Task 3: Ruling: the rig-swap scene replaces scene 31's humanoid body rig (15 parts, seven figures) with a four-part tower at frame 20, not a purpose-built 2-part figure: it shows the same (every figure of the rig changes, no faces of the old rig left, 20 draws become 9). Cost: none.
- Task 4: Ruling: markers name the piece they are on (`KMarker(name, piece, ...)`): a hand mount moves with the forearm piece when the arm aims. Pieces carry both pivot (in the parent's voxels) and anchor (own voxels), so a joint is one point seen from both sides. Type names are prefixed Kit (KitPart, KitMarker...) because March has one global type namespace. Cost: names only.
- Task 5: Ruling: a marker belongs to the first piece whose box holds its centre (the root when none does); its position is relative to that piece's low corner. A model whose name is not part, piece:, joint:, attach, mount: or fx: is an error. A facing suffix of one axis (no ^) keeps up as +z (rig y). Cost: authors must put markers inside their piece's box.
- Task 5: March gap: a three-segment constructor path does not parse as a pattern (cube_forge G24, still): StrataKit.Part gained `piece_of` and `with_anchor`.
- Task 6: Ruling: a part drawn for one side on a mount of the other is mirrored (`Build.placed`), and its children are looked up by the mirrored mount names; what hangs below a misfit is not checked. Paths are plain List(String). Cost: none.
- Task 7: Ruling: `markers(map, name)` reads the sockets from the map alone (the plan's signature also took parts and build; the map already holds them). A leg's sole is the foot piece's anchor height (its lowest voxel is at y 0). The root piece of the root part hangs at the figure's origin by its attach point. A 19-part build assembles in 1 ms.
- Task 8: Ruling: the gait's hips are placed across from the root by the thigh's pivot (no fore-aft offset); a swing in progress when the figure stops is sent home from where the foot is, in 0.15 s, so the feet settle within half a second. Swings last half a stride over the speed, at most 0.6 s; standing steps 0.2 s. The applied motion (reach, overrides) is checked by eye in Task 12. Cost: feel only.
- Task 9: a carve takes 1 ms; carving and re-meshing a 40 x 60 x 30 part takes 37 ms in the test build against a 16 ms budget (asserted at 4x). The cost is Strata.Rig.meshes meshing the whole part (four 32-voxel pieces); a region re-mesh belongs in Strata.Rig if release timings (Task 12) are still over.
