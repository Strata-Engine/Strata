# Rulings made while building map generation
- Task 3: Ruling: flatten eases the margin from the cells' bounding box (Chebyshev distance), not from each cell: footprints are rectangles, and per-cell distance would cost cells x area. Cost: an L-shaped footprint eases from its box.
- Task 10: Ruling (slice): MapData holds only what the slice's stages need (heights, water, weirs, sinks, reports); roads, lots, buildings, pieces, trees, lava and sites join it with their tasks. The map is centred on the origin. Cost: none.
- Task 11: Ruling: each recipe's noise is stretched to [0, 1] over the square before shaping, so relief R is reached exactly (highlands span about R); canyon is a plateau at 0.85-1.0 R cut by a winding channel of 16% of the square's width; plateau is four terraced levels. Cost: a map's relief no longer varies with the seed's luck.
- Task 12: Ruling (slice): rivers always run west to east across the whole square; lakes (Strata.Flood basins at water >= 0.5) are not in the slice. A stretch keeps its level until the running level (the lower bank less 2, never rising downstream) has fallen more than 2 voxels; a weir wall stands across the river at that column. Banks are eased down to the water over 3 columns. Cost: every river crosses the map the same way round.
- Task 12: March: `init` is reserved (the actor initialiser) and cannot name a variable; `with` cannot name a function. For GAPS.
- Task 18: Ruling (slice): the generator writes terrain and water only (stone, dirt 3 deep, grass or sand under water, water, weirs, sinks); templates (buildings, pieces, trees) join with their tasks. A chunk wholly under the lowest ground less 4 is uniform stone, wholly over the highest ground or water uniform air. Cost: none.
- Task 21 (slice): Ruling: the check runs on the height field (tile centre heights, water depth) with fords standing in for bridges; the voxel-world check comes with buildings — cost if wrong: a map passes here but blocks once sites and props land, caught when Task 21 is finished.
- Task 21 (slice): Ruling: the river centre is kept in the playable square's middle three quarters and the fords sit at a third and two thirds of the playable square — seed 8 had run its river through the ring, leaving the playable square dry — cost if wrong: rivers can no longer hug the map edge.
- Task 22 (slice): one example, plugins/strata_maps/example: generate, stream, tactics camera, scripted shot docs/maps-slice.png. Seed 5 passes on the first attempt with 2 routes in about 0.5 s (release). Seen in the picture: the ring's easing makes square contour lines outside the playable square; to be smoothed when the ring gets its own pass.
- Task 1: Strata.Trees in the library; build(species, seed, voxel, wood, leaves) draws in ids 1 and 2 and remaps at the end; base_offset is half. Golden images unchanged.
- Task 2: Strata.Edit.stamp blits into a scratch chunk of a sentinel id (65535) per touched chunk and passes the written cells to set_many.
- Task 4: Ruling: Roads.at answers (segment, metres along); the surface comes from segments. Ruling: adapt and chunk_paint take no water: a road column more than 2 voxels above its ground is a gap adapt leaves and chunk_paint decks (a dry gully gets a viaduct); bridges reports water crossings with the banks' height. Node heights are eased over 16 passes so no segment exceeds the grade.
- Task 5: Ruling: Lots.blocks covers free land with greedy rectangles (a run in x grown in z), not maximal rectangles; a lot faces the side with the most road beside it.
