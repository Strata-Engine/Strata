How a Steel Rain mech is put together

The core (grey) stays the same. Everything blue or orange is swapped, and the mech's look changes with it. Yellow dots are mount points.

Anatomy: one core, its mount points, and what fills them

Same core, three builds

Assault

Legs · rifle and shield in the hands · autocannon on the shoulder · missile pod on the back

Siege

Treads · gun-arms (the weapon is the forearm, no hands) · missile pod

Scout

Legs · one light gun in the hand · jump jets on the back · free shoulders

Which way should parts fit?

A

Universal by mount size (recommended)

Every mount is Light, Medium or Heavy. Any part of the right size fits any chassis: a Medium arm fits a light core's Medium shoulder and a heavy core's Medium shoulder alike. More combinations, more loot variety, art reused across chassis. Art follows attachment rules.

B

Chassis-specific

Each chassis has its own family of arms, legs and back pieces, designed to match. Looks more coherent, but needs much more art for the same variety, and loot per chassis is thinner. Weapons could still be shared.
