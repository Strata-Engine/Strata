Task 3: Measurement (bench/populate_fields under March 0.7.0, load average 67 to 88; modes 4 and 6 at REPS=200000, modes 5, 7, 8 at REPS=2000 to 4000, two runs each): `place_with` with no layers costs 0.1 us of CPU and 0.5 k instructions a call; with one cover layer on a chunk no floor reaches, 0.1 us and 0.4 k (Review Focus 4: under 5 us).
