Task 4: Ruling: an instance stands on the column holding its place, `floor(xm / voxel)`, `floor(zm / voxel)`; its density, its top and its weights zone are read there. Its template, turned (`w` by `d` columns), is blitted from `w / 2` and `d / 2` columns before that column (integer division) and one voxel above the column's top.
