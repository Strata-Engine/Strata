Task 4: Ruling: an instance stands on the column holding its place, `floor(xm / voxel)`, `floor(zm / voxel)`; its density, its top and its weights zone are read there. Its template, turned (`w` by `d` columns), is blitted from `w / 2` and `d / 2` columns before that column (integer division) and one voxel above the column's top. So it covers at most `floor(w / 2)` columns either side, never more than `reach / voxel`, and `pad` (one more) is enough at every scale — implementer's call; for an odd side this is `Strata.Template`'s and the viewer's convention (`wx - half`), for an even side (every template fitted by an even factor) the base column is the one just past the middle, so a quarter turn does not keep one template voxel on it — a game that wants a fitted trunk exactly centred needs an odd fitted size, which fitting by 2 or 4 cannot give.

Task 6: Ruling: `pad` (and `plan_pad`) is the largest of the scatter pad `ceil(reach / scale) + 1` and every layer's need, worked out over the run order from the last layer back: a cover layer needs its density whole over the chunk (0 columns beyond), a scatter layer over the scatter pad, a site layer nowhere; a layer needing e columns that reads a cover field (through its rule's curves and zones, and for cover and scatter layers its weights zones, through curve fields, bands and combined zones) makes that field's source layer need `e + ceil(r / scale)` when it scatters, e when it is a cover layer, nothing when it is a site layer. So a cover layer reading a scatter layer's cover of radius r needs r (the pad is max(scatter pad, ceil(r / scale))), a scatter layer reading it needs the scatter pad plus r, and chains add up. The source's own template reach does not add: a base is counted by its position, not its stamp. `ceil(r / scale)` is enough because a base within r of a column's centre stands on a column at most floor(r / scale + 0.5) away.
