# Strata main: free-standing voxel grids (survey for fine plant grids)

Source: `git show main:<path>` only. Paths are in lib/strata unless noted. Design line numbers are from `cat -n` of main's docs/2026-11-11-physics-design.md (PD), -entities-design.md (ED), -animation-design.md (AD), -physics-plan.md (PP).
Scratch copy of physics.march: scratchpad/physics.march.txt (line numbers below for physics.march refer to it = main's file).

## 1. What a Strata.Body is today

- `Strata.Body` (body.march, 304 lines) is only the grid and its derived data, not the body. `Grid = Grid(sx, sy, sz, NativeI32Arr)`, dense block ids (registry ids), x fastest (body.march:17). `max_side()` = 128 voxels per axis (body.march:14); `grid()` clamps each side to 1..128 (:21-27). PD:130 says the same ("up to 128 voxels a side (12.8 m at 10 cm, 128 m at 1 m)"); larger loose pieces are split along 128-voxel lines (PD:131, physics.march `split128` ~:818).
- Derived data: `mass_of(registry, grid, scale)` gives mass, centre, inertia tensor from per-block density, each voxel a uniform cube (body.march:69-123); `surface` (colliding voxels with an open face, :151); `corners` (lattice corners on the surface, the contact points, :183); `split` (face-connected parts, each in its own bounding box, :272); `from_world(world, cells)` (:295).
- `Strata.Physics.Rigid` record (physics.march:60-63): id, kind (dynamic 0 / driven 1), grid, surf (corner indices), mass m, centre lx ly lz, inverse inertia ii, position of centre, quaternion q, v, w, still counter, asleep, edge, waited, driven half-extents hx hy hz, ground, previous pose (for interpolation). Bodies are held in `Physics.bodies : List(Rigid)` (physics.march:95), found by linear `find` by id; ids never reused.
- **Scale**: `scale` is NOT a field of the body. It is one metres-per-voxel number in `PhysConfig` (last field, `with_scale`, physics.march:31-40, 49). Its doc: "Metres per voxel: must be the world's scale. A body's mass depends on it." body.march header: "A body's voxels are the world's size; `scale` is metres per voxel" (body.march:3-4). PD:140: "A body's voxels are the world's size: a body in a 10 cm world has 10 cm voxels." Every use (bounds, contact generation, support spans, to_blocks snapping, drawing) reads the one `c_scale(p.cfg)`. So a body's voxel size cannot differ from the world's, and cannot differ between bodies.
- **Built vs planned** (git log on main of physics.march/body.march): dba0ec7 Task 1 (materials, body grids, mass/inertia); d6363b1 Task 2 (against the world, sleep); 24c1472 Task 3 (body-body, stacking, awake/total caps); 7ce4426 Task 4 (worker steps, clock, interpolation, driven bodies, body-aware queries); b6e01a4 Task 5 (drawn at pose, true normals, 10 cm viewer world); bc1290c Task 6 (shadow/sky/reflection rays see bodies); 370b42f Task 7 (support by connection and span; loose terrain becomes bodies), merged to main as ce256ae. **Tasks 8 (breaking: impact fracture, fragments snap or spray) and 9 (water: bodies as walls, sinking, splash) are not on main**: no fracture.march in lib/strata (ls-tree), no commit. There is no physics ledger in docs/ (ledgers: animation, audio, entities, fluid, generation, lighting, renderer, ui, voxel-core). The plan has 9+ tasks (PP task headings: 1 materials/grids, 2 one body vs world, 3 bodies vs bodies+caps, 4 worker/clock/driven, 5 drawing, 6 rays, 7 support, 8 breaking, 9 water; later 10 is scenes/soak).
- Mass uses per-block `density` (default 2000), plus `strength` (50 000 J/m2), `span` (4 m), `piece size` (0.5 m), `anchor` from `Strata.Blocks.with_physics` (PD:153-159). Blocks that do not collide (plants, fluid) carry no mass, surface or connection: "a plant inside a grid is carried but weighs nothing" (body.march:7-8); PD:163-164: "Blocks that do not collide (plants, fluid) ... are never part of a body: a body made from terrain leaves them behind, and a plant whose ground is taken is removed by the same edit."
- Body voxels are registry blocks (textured, light class), not palette colours (AD:378-379).

For fine plant grids this means: a 0.25 m body in a 1 m world is impossible today (one global scale, mass, contact, support, snap and draw all assume world voxels), so fine plant grids either need per-body scale threaded through Physics/Contact, or must live outside Physics until they are felled; wood/leaf blocks would need density/strength/span values meant for 0.25 m voxels.

## 2. Strata.Support

- support.march (267 lines). Header (:1-22): "what an edit leaves without support, and so comes loose." Works in a window round the edit: the edits' bounds grown by the longest span of any colliding block plus 2 voxels (PD:174-179). Cells are air/solid/anchor (anchor = registry anchor flag, any block at or below `anchor_y`, any cell of an unloaded chunk). Seeds: anchors and solid cells on the window boundary. Each solid cell gets a distance in voxels from support: resting on a cell inherits its distance, beside or hanging is +1; held when distance <= its material's span (`span_vox`, converts cm span with `scale`, :43). Unsupported cells face-connected to each other form one loose piece (`Loose`, `pieces`, `capped`; :28-33, :239-264). Fill cap 200 000 voxels (:36); larger pieces are counted as held. So yes: it detects unsupported voxels/pieces in the **world grid** and also overhangs past span even when joined to the whole world (PD:186-192).
- Trigger: off by default; `with_support(cfg, true, anchor_y)` (physics.march:37). `Physics.wake(p, outcome)` queues an edit's `Strata.Edit.changes` (physics.march:226). In the step, `support_stage` (physics.march ~:855-875) runs `Strata.Support.loose(reg, w, p.wakes, anchor_y, scale)` only if support is on and wakes is non-empty; pieces are split at 128 voxels (`split128`), each becomes a dynamic body at rest through `loose_body` (add with identity rotation at the piece's low corner), and the cells go to air through one `Strata.Edit.set_many` (plus the plant standing on top, `leave_one`). Event kind 2 "loose" is emitted. Target: starts to fall within 3 steps of the edit; support pass <= 10 ms for a small 10 cm edit (PD:385-386).
- Support only reads world blocks. "Bodies never hold up the world" (PD:89, 171). A body is never in the support window, and nothing in Support looks at bodies or entity grids.
- Stale results (edits made between begin and finish) are redone, not applied (PD:265-268), though plan Task 4 notes that handling is a Task 7 concern.

For fine plant grids this means: Support is the right mechanism for "tree on 1 m ground falls when its base is cut" only if the tree is world voxels; a plant that is its own grid needs its own support pass over its own fine grid (connectivity to its root voxels, which is `Strata.Body.split` generalised with an anchor), and an edit must be routed to the plant instead of the world; Support's span table is in 1 m-world units (`span_vox` uses the single scale).

## 3. Creation, drawing, collision, edit, return to world

- **Lift world voxels into a body**: yes, but only as the by-product of support. `Strata.Body.from_world(w, cells)` copies the world's block ids at a cell list into a bounding-box grid (body.march:295-303, clips beyond 128 voxels of the first cell). `Physics.loose_body` (physics.march ~:866) calls it, `add`s the body at the box corner, and the same step removes the cells from the world as an edit. There is no public "lift these voxels now" call: a game cannot say "make this selection a body", it can only (a) build a Grid itself and `Physics.add(p, grid, x, y, z, quat, kind)` (physics.march:145, "a body from blocks the game supplies", PD:359), or (b) trigger support. Nothing marks a plant as removable except the support/edit path.
- **Draw**: the body grid is meshed by the ordinary mesher in 32-cubed parts (`part_count`, `part_offset`, `part_key` level 99; physics.march:1276-1293; `take_meshes` :1345). `Strata.Render.bodies(r, p, t)` (render.march:167) uploads parts via `Vk.body_begin` + `chunk_part` + `chunk_commit` into the same chunk slots as the world (the shared 16 384; PD:246-248), poses each slot with `Vk.slot_pose(slot, low corner x y z, quaternion)` (render.march:144-163, ffi/vk.march:103). The pose has **no scale parameter**; the part's offset is multiplied by `Physics.scale(p)` and quads are drawn at the world voxel size. Body is drawn between its last two finished states, one step behind (PD:276-278). Remeshed only when the grid changes (PD:324-326).
- **Terrain collision**: contacts use the lattice corners of surface voxels (`Body.corners`) against a world window copied once per step (Contact `Win3`, one byte per cell; contact.march:30-60, 99-111); speculative contacts, 8 sequential-impulse passes, push-out limited to 0.2 voxel/step, sub-steps so no more than half a voxel per sub-step (PD:199-215, PP Task 2). All of it in world voxel coordinates `floor(p / scale)`: body corners are tested against world cells; another body's grid is tested through `grid_window` in body-voxel coordinates (contact.march:257-265, 340). Body-vs-body uses the same corner scheme at the single scale. Driven bodies are axis-aligned boxes (physics.march:1026).
- **Edit a body's voxels (carve)**: not built. Physics never mutates a body's grid after creation: no `grid_set` on a `Rigid` anywhere in physics.march (`Body.grid_set` exists, body.march:49, but nothing calls it on a live body). Hit tests on bodies give a voxel index in the body's grid (`Physics.raycast`, physics.march:1237) but nothing writes it back. PD:225-228 plans fracture (Task 8): impact over strength cuts the grid into piece-sized cells and spawns bodies for each piece (seed points from a hash of body id and step); not on main. PD:207: "Explosions or tools as features" are non-goals: a game makes them from edits and `push`. There is no blast API and no per-voxel carve of a body.
- **Put back in the world**: `Physics.to_blocks(p, w, id)` (physics.march:764): body is turned to the nearest of the 24 axis rotations, voxels rounded to whole world voxels, written by `Edit.set_many`; a voxel whose target cell holds a colliding block is dropped; then the body is removed. Automatic form: past the total cap (4 096) the sleeping bodies farthest from the focus and out of view are snapped (`capped`, :776). Fragments (< 8 voxels) snapping onto ground at rest and spray are Task 8, not on main (PD:229-234). This is lossy: a 0.25 m grid cannot be snapped into 1 m cells.
- Water reaction (bodies as walls, sinking) is Task 9, not built.

For fine plant grids this means: the pieces to reuse are Grid, from_world-style lifting, the part-meshing and slot drawing, rays-see-bodies and the drive/sweep queries; what must be added is a body scale (draw, contact, mass, snap), a "make this grid a body now" path that is not support-driven, and per-voxel carving of a live grid (clear a voxel, re-run `Body.split`, remesh the touched 32-cubed part, hand each remaining part to Physics as a new body).

## 4. Lighting and shadows of bodies and figures

- The world light volume is a world-scale one-byte-per-voxel 3D texture, written in whole chunks (lighting design lines ~82-103; PD:515-518 call-out). **Bodies are never written into it** (PD:324 "never writes the world's light volume"; PD "calls made" 7, PD:488-490). Instead each body's `classes(p, id)` (one light-class byte per voxel, physics.march:1331) is uploaded with `Vk.body_shape(handle, bytes, sx, sy, sz)` and posed with `Vk.body_place` (ffi/vk.march:98-100; render.march:195-205). The shim (native/strata_vk.c:131-136) builds each frame a coarse world-aligned grid, one cell per chunk, listing bodies overlapping it; shadow, sky and reflection rays that enter a listed cell step through each body's grid in its own frame (native/shaders/trace.glsl:36-46, `body_ray` :100-140). So bodies are lit with true normals (octahedral normal written only for turned surfaces; render ledger Task 6 ruling), cast exact tilted shadows, self-shadow, appear in reflections (PD:333-340).
- Limits in the shim: SV_MAX_BODIES 16 384 handles; 64 MB of class bytes in all (SV_BODY_WORDS 16<<20 words = 64 MB, :135); 262 144 body-list entries per frame (:136); 16 "mover" boxes a frame to unsettle light history (:137; physics-path boxes), which the figure code replaces with per-figure per-pixel flags because "the physics path's 16 world boxes cannot hold a crowd" (animation ledger Task 6). A cell listing is read by every ray crossing it; PD:457-459 flags many bodies per cell as a GPU risk (target: 100 bodies in view add <= 1 ms).
- **The ray shader already supports a per-grid voxel size**: `BodyRow.pos.w` is "voxel size (0: the world's)" and `body_ray` computes `sc = pos.w > 0 ? pos.w : u.eye.w` and `kv = sc / u.eye.w` (trace.glsl:42, 108-109). It is used by figures (rig parts at their own voxel size); plain bodies set 0.
- **Figures** (Strata.Anim / Strata.Rig, AD): a rig has a "Voxel size in metres, per rig, independent of the world's voxel scale. A 1.8 m figure at 1/16 m voxels is 29 voxels tall" (AD:191-192). Palette colours (255 entries with roughness and emission), not world blocks (AD:193-198, 378-383). Up to 64 parts per rig, each grid up to 128 per axis (AD:199-203). Drawing: pieces of 32 cubed in a table of their own (16 384 pieces, SV_FIG_PIECES) beside the world's quad buffer, pipeline of its own, one instanced draw per piece per rig part, shared by every figure of the rig; poses are a 3x4 matrix a part from C (AD:368-377; animation ledger Task 6). Light, per figure's choice: **plain** (default): lit by the world, casts sun shadows through a shadow map of plain figures only; not in reflections, does not darken sky light. **Traced** (`Strata.Anim.traced`): parts join the ray list, traced shadow, reflections, sky occlusion, emission lights surroundings; **capped at 64 at once** (SV_FIG_TRACED, strata_vk.c:1242; AD:405-408), extras drawn plain and counted (AD:384-408). With lighting off all figures are plain. Both built (animation ledger Tasks 6, 7, 8 on main: figures drawn, plain shadows, traced figures).
- Light history: a moving figure is flagged by a checksum of its matrices and its pixels settle with memory capped at 2 samples; a body resets a world-space box (animation ledger Task 6 rulings). A static figure that never moves does not reset history.

For fine plant grids this means: a plain figure is the cheap lit/shadowed path (shared palette mesh, shadow map, instanced, many thousands of parts) and a physics-body-style ray path is the expensive exact path (per-cell lists, 64 MB of bytes, 16 384 handles); a plant as static scenery would be a figure with its voxel size 0.25 m (trace.glsl already has the scale hook), a plant as a falling body would need the body path's block-class bytes at a body scale; neither path puts a plant's leaves into the world's light volume, so sky-light occlusion under a canopy only exists for traced figures (cap 64) or if plants stay world voxels.

## 5. Strata.Entities

- entities.march (418 lines) over a C kernel (native/strata_ent.c, Strata.Ffi.Ent). An entity is a row of columns, reached by integer id (generation<<20 | row): kind, position (doubles, metres), velocity, facing (one angle about vertical), age, 8 float slots, 8 int slots, and part ids (figure, body, voice 0, voice 1) (ED:131-161; entities ledger Task 1 ruling: ~185 bytes a row). A `Kind` carries a mover (`placed` = "Not moved by Strata: the game sets its position", `flying`, `walking`, `bodied`), a box (hx hy hz), step height, gravity, lifetime, seen distance, unseen rule (lives/freezes/goes with divisor), slot counts (entities.march:48-80). Placed entities exist, so **static placed objects could be entities**; an entity itself holds no voxels: its parts are a figure id (an `Strata.Anim` figure) and optionally a body.
- Body part: ED:196-207: a body mover is a driven box via `Physics.add_driven`, or a `Strata.Body.Grid` becoming a dynamic body via `Physics.add`; "A body exists only while the entity is seen" (ED:202). The `bodied` mover exists as a constant but entities.march:68 says "(the body comes with a later `with_` function)": the body mover is entities plan Task 8, not on main (entities ledger covers Tasks 1-6; git log shows Tasks 4, 5, 6 merges).
- Budgets: capacity 65 536 rows by default, max 1 048 576 (entities.march:116 doc; ED:131); body budget at `open` default 128 (entities.march:116; ED:245-251, bodies given to nearest entities that want one; game's own bodies outside it); figures live within `Strata.Anim`'s 4 096 (ED:123-124, 90); at most 8 float and 8 int slots a kind. Seen only within (radius_h - 2) chunks of a focus (entities.march:116). Targets (ED:385-395): 20 000 flying entities seen + touch pairs 2 ms; 2 000 walkers with figures 4 ms; 10 000 unseen walkers on routes (divisor 4) 0.5 ms; spawn/remove 5 000 in a tick 1 ms. Kernel holds a one-bit-per-voxel copy of the loaded blocks (ED:355-366), set where blocks collide, whole cell for model blocks.
- Spatial queries: after each tick every live entity is put in a uniform grid of 4 m cells (entities.march:35-37; ED:272). `near(point, radius, kind mask)` returns ids sorted within a radius (entities.march:323); `raycast(origin, dir, max, mask)` returns the first entity whose **box** the ray meets plus distance (:334; ED:276-278). Exact voxel hits are then asked of `Strata.Anim` with the entity's figure (ED:277-278: `hit_ray` returns figure, part, voxel, distance, face normal, palette entry; AD:326-333). Both answer as of the last `step`: not found until the next step after adding.
- Entities run seen/unseen switching: an entity near a focus becomes seen and is given its figure, voices and (later) body; leaving view removes them (ED:222-240).

For fine plant grids this means: a plant instance could be a `placed` entity (position, facing, slots for species/age/seed) with a figure made from a shared rig at 0.25 m, giving near/raycast by box and exact voxel ray via Anim.hit_ray; but the 4 m query cells and the box-only entity raycast are coarse for large trees, the figure budget is 4 096 live figures (parts drawn up to 16 384 in a draw list), and carving a rig shared by all instances would need per-instance grids (a shared rig is by design identical across figures).

## 6. Limits and budgets stated

- Body grid: 128 voxels a side (body.march:14; PD:130). Fill cap for support 200 000 voxels (support.march:36). Fragment: < 8 voxels (PD:229).
- Bodies: 256 awake and handled per step nearest the focus, rest wait (PD:236-238; config default `PhConfig(9.8, 60, 8, 256, 4096, false, -64, 1.0)`, physics.march:34: gravity 9.8, 60 steps/s, 8 solver passes, 256 awake, 4096 total, support off, anchor -64, scale 1.0). Total 4 096, past it far sleeping out-of-view bodies are snapped into the grid; excess reported (PD:244-251). Renderer slots shared with world chunks: 16 384 in all, ~3 100 used by the viewer's world (PD:246-248). Body rays: 16 384 handles, 64 MB classes, 262 144 list entries (strata_vk.c:131-137).
- Cost: PD:375-393 targets (M3 Max, release): worst frame 16.7 ms; main-thread physics <= 1 ms; worker step: 10 cm wall (8x3x0.3 m, about 7 200 voxels) <= 8 ms; 10 cm tower crumbling (100 awake bodies) <= 16 ms; 1 m cliff face (about 50 bodies) <= 8 ms; support pass after a small 10 cm edit <= 10 ms (window ~90 voxels a side); start falling within 3 steps; asleep within 3 s; no tunnelling at 30 m/s; GPU added by 100 bodies in view <= 1 ms; 160 000-voxel 10 cm cliff measured not promised. PP Task 2 Step 4: if one step of a 2 m crate at 10 cm costs more than 4 ms, stop and ask about a C kernel (physics risk, PD:451-456: persistent-map work per cell cost about 5 us in fluid, too slow; solver stays on native arrays in March on a worker).
- No ledger records measured numbers for physics (no physics-ledger.md on main; PP asks for them in "the ledger"; the animation and entities ledgers hold only their own rulings). Treat all physics budgets as targets, unverified from docs.
- Figures: 4 096 in Strata.Anim (ED:123); 64 parts a rig, 128 per axis (AD:199-203); 64 traced at once; 16 384 parts in a draw list and 16 384 figure pieces (SV_FIG_PIECES/INST); 512 figures x 15 parts update <= 1 ms; figure pass 1.5 ms; shadow map 1.0 ms; traced 64 humanoids 1.5 ms; arena 120 fps (AD:490-512).
- Entities: see 5.

For fine plant grids this means: budgets assume a few hundred active bodies of tens of thousands of voxels at most; a forest's static plants must be off the body list (figures, or a world-side form) and only a handful of felled plants can be live bodies at once; a 0.25 m 128-voxel grid is 32 m, adequate for a tree but 8 times the voxel count per metre cubed compared to 1 m world.

## 7. Different voxel size, sub-voxel detail, props

- Bodies: explicitly the same as the world's (above). PD:4 decision: "Both voxel scales, 1 m and 10 cm" means the whole world (and its bodies) at one of two scales (a `STRATA_SCALE=0.1` viewer), not mixed.
- Figures: per-rig voxel size independent of the world (AD:191-192, example 1/16 m voxels in a 1 m world; viewer humanoid at 1/16 m, animation ledger Task 6: "a part needs its own voxel size (1/16 m in a 1 m world)"). `Vk.rig_part_anchor(rig, part, ax, ay, az, voxel)` carries the rig's voxel size to the renderer; `Strata.Rig` parts clamp at 128 per axis. So sub-world-voxel detail on moving or placed objects exists only as figures (rigid parts, palette colours, no deformation: "Part scale is not animated", AD:92-93), and as `.vox` import for templates via `Strata.Template.from_vox` (template.march header; native/ogt_vox.h), which stamps into the world's voxels.
- Props: no "props" concept in the physics, entities or animation designs; the nearest are placed-mover entities and figures (a "placed" kind, ED), and procedural features: `Strata.Feature` (hashed cells, templates stamped into chunks at world voxels; feature.march header) and `Strata.Template` (a block of voxels with per-voxel and per-row probabilities, blitted into chunks) which build today's generated trees in the world grid at world voxel size (generation ledger; viewer TreeGrid). Procedural trees drawn at world scale have "coarse voxels draw no thin branches" (commit bb42f4f). `Strata.Tree` in tree.march is a generic persistent binary tree data structure, not vegetation.
- PD:156-163 (materials): span and piece size are in metres "so the same material behaves alike at either scale"; a finer-than-world body would need that metre meaning, which exists for support (`span_vox`, `scale`) and for contact sizes only through the single scale.
- Entities design sets no voxel size on the entity; `Entities.open(..., chunk_metres, voxel_metres)` takes the world's (ED:294).
- Task 8's fracture (PD:228-234) cuts bodies into pieces at material piece size; fragments of one voxel turn to spray: nothing for sub-voxel debris.

For fine plant grids this means: the only existing sub-world voxel size path is the figure/rig pipeline (and the shader's `pos.w`), so a plant at 0.25 m should start as an Anim figure per species-instance (or a rig per plant) and be converted to a Physics body only when felled, which requires adding a body scale to Physics; it is not "props" in any design.
